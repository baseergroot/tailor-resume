# Brag Plan: HireFit — AI Resume Tailor

## What is it (one sentence)?
HireFit lets you paste a job description and upload your resume to get an ATS-optimized tailored resume, score, and cover letter in 30 seconds — first one free, no signup required.

## Who is it for, and what does it do for them?
Job seekers (software engineers, PMs, designers, marketers) who keep getting ghosted by ATS filters. HireFit rewrites their actual experience to match the JD's exact keywords — preserving every metric and truth — so humans actually see their resume.

## What sets it apart?
- **Preservation-first**: Never invents skills, metrics, or experience. Only uses JD keywords truthfully supported by the original resume.
- **Anonymous-first flow**: First tailoring works without signup. Second requires Google login (resume saved to account).
- **6 AI tools in one**: JD Analyzer, Resume Analyzer, Gap Analyzer, ATS Scorer, Resume Rewriter, Cover Letter Generator.
- **Real streaming SSE**: Tool-by-tool progress visible in ~30-60s.

## Most impressive claim
"Went from 0 callbacks to 3 final rounds in two weeks" — Sarah Chen, Senior SWE at Stripe
